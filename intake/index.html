How it works

Three moving parts, no jargon

A short enquiry form sits on your website, in your colours. Name, email, date, guests and a message — nothing more.

02

Each submission is appended to the spreadsheet you already use, with a timestamp and a clean status column.

03

Gmail auto-reply + notification

The customer gets a friendly reply in seconds, and a notification lands on your phone so nothing slips through.
